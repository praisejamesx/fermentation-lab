SG is for beer, wine, mead, and kombucha; leave it blank for vegetable ferments. The verdict uses the structured visible-fault choice, pH, temperature, ferment type, and target. Free-text notes are context, not keyword triggers.

What this cannot decide

  • It cannot certify a batch safe. pH, time, temperature, recipe, and visible evidence decide.
  • It cannot identify a pathogen or a toxin. A pleasant smell is not a test.
  • It cannot compare across arrays or rigs. The sensor panel is same-array only.
  • It cannot measure concentration from a MOX response. The array sees a mixture.
  • The array phase is a shape estimate. A sealed ferment displaces oxygen with CO₂, which can flatten the array without the fermentation stopping.
